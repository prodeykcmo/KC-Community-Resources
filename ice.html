Know your rights (general)

  • You have the right to remain silent.
  • You do not have to open your door unless presented with a valid warrant signed by a judge.
  • You can ask for an interpreter.

If ICE is nearby

  • Stay calm and prioritize safety.
  • Contact a trusted legal organization as soon as possible.
  • Write down details: date/time, location, badge numbers (if visible), and what occurred.

What not to do

  • Do not share personal information online.
  • Do not post names, addresses, or private details in public channels.
